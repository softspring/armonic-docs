# Why Armonic?

Armonic is a CMS built for teams that want to move fast without sacrificing the flexibility of Symfony.

It was created after years of building Symfony projects and seeing the same gap repeatedly: teams need a CMS that is easy for content editors, but still clean and extensible for developers.

## Built for Symfony projects {#built-for-symfony}

Armonic runs natively in Symfony, so you can keep a single architecture and a single deployment flow.

- Reuse Symfony bundles, components, and conventions.
- Keep your project secure and maintainable with a proven framework.
- Extend behavior with custom modules and integrations when your project grows.

## Why teams choose Armonic {#why-teams-choose-armonic}

Armonic focuses on practical features that improve day-to-day work for both technical and non-technical teams.

### Faster content operations

- Inline editing directly on the page.
- Module editor for structured content workflows.
- Live preview before publishing.
- Publish/unpublish control by page and version.

### Safe and collaborative publishing

- Version history for every content change.
- Clear traceability of who changed what and when.
- Easy rollback to previous versions.

### Multi-site and multi-language by design

- Manage multiple websites from one platform.
- Deliver localized content with a powerful translation workflow.
- Reuse shared blocks and content patterns across pages and sites.

### SEO and performance ready

- Built-in SEO fields for metadata management.
- Optimized architecture aligned with Symfony performance practices.
- Prepared for future AI-assisted workflows.

## Is Armonic for me? {#is-it-for-me}

Armonic is a strong fit if you:

- Build on Symfony (new or existing projects).
- Need marketers and editors to update content without constant developer intervention.
- Want to scale from a single site to multi-site/multi-language setups.

If you are not a developer, Armonic is still usable for day-to-day content editing, but a developer is needed to set up and maintain the project.

## Armonic flavours {#armonic-flavours}

You can adopt Armonic in different ways depending on your project:

- **Armonic standalone**: start from a ready-to-run base project.
- **Armonic for existing Symfony projects**: install only the bundles and components you need.
- **Armonic for Sylius**: integrate Armonic into your Sylius-based stack.
